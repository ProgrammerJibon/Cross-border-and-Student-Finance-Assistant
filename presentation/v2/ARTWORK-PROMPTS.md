# Illustration prompt set

Mode: built-in `image_gen.imagegen`; no CLI/API fallback.

## Cover — `assets/finance-before-after.png`

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: premium widescreen hero illustration for the FinBridge AI student-finance presentation. Create one refined minimal editorial vector-style illustration, wide landscape, on a flat deep midnight-navy background #080F1B. The picture must communicate a problem becoming a solution without any words. LEFT: an international university student at a small desk, looking concerned at a tuition invoice, a rent bill, a calendar with a looming deadline, and disconnected wallet/currency cards. A small family video-call window shows a parent trying to help. Papers and paths look fragmented but deliberately sparse. RIGHT: the same student visibly calm beside one coherent laptop financial workspace showing a simple tuition progress bar, one clear funding path from a parent wallet to a university building, and a small approval check. Connect the two scenes with one elegant mint bridge/flow line. Use generous negative space, bold easily recognized silhouettes, flat geometric shapes, a little soft dimensional shading, precise clean edges, restrained mint #63E6BE, blue #6BB9FF, amber #F3C969 and coral #FF8896 accents. Human characters should be tasteful contemporary illustration, not photorealistic or childish. Only a few meaningful objects. Put a moderate empty margin around all artwork so it can be placed on a slide. No text, letters, logos, numbers, watermarks, title, decorative charts, floating random symbols, gradients behind the full scene, or busy background. The viewer should instantly understand stressed student with fragmented funding becoming calm student with an organized tuition funding plan.

## Problem statement — `assets/student-funding-problem.png`

Reference: the generated cover illustration above, used as a style reference rather than an edit target.

Use case: illustration-story. The supplied image is a STYLE REFERENCE, not an edit target. Create a separate, minimal problem-statement illustration for the same FinBridge AI presentation. Match its polished editorial human illustration, midnight navy #080F1B background, blue clothing, restrained mint and amber accents. Landscape 3:2 composition. Show only the unresolved problem: the same international student seated at a desk holding a tuition invoice with a graduation-cap symbol, worried because a rent bill with a house symbol and an imminent deadline calendar compete for the same small wallet. In the upper left a small simple parent-and-home vignette connects to that wallet through a broken/dashed cross-border path; on the right a university building waits behind the looming calendar. Keep the hierarchy exceptionally clear: STUDENT + TWO BILLS + LIMITED WALLET + BROKEN FAMILY FUNDING PATH. One student, minimal objects, plenty of negative space around the vignette, high quality clean edges, no decorative graphs. No solution, checks, completed transfers, text, letters, numbers, labels, logos or watermark. The viewer must understand 'my family has money in another country, but can I cover rent AND tuition by the deadline?' at a glance. Keep all visual elements away from the outer 5 percent of the canvas.
